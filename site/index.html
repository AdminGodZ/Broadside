Then run it

./broadside play --heat

play is a game against the engine; --heat shows where it expects your ships. assist tells you where to fire in a game against anyone else. On Windows, use broadside.exe.

On a Mac, the first launch is blocked because the app isn't signed with an Apple developer ID. Allow it in System Settings under Privacy & Security.

How it works

It counts every fleet

Most bots guess where ships might be. Broadside counts every layout that fits the hits, misses and sinkings so far, exactly, and fires where a ship is most likely.

It plays the endgame out

When few enough layouts remain, it plays each candidate shot to the end in every one of them and picks the shot that finishes fastest.

It learns how you play

Over a session it learns where you like to hide ships and which waters you search last, so the games get harder the longer you play.
